An irregularly regular publication from your friendly neighbourhood Nihal.

Dear Subscriber,

Congratulations. You have officially been enrolled in a very exclusive publication.

Following the apparent unavailability of telephone-based communication, the management has decided to introduce a far more sophisticated alternative: letters.

Yes, actual letters. The sort that existed before social media.

Your subscription includes occasional stories from my week, random thoughts that probably deserve to remain inside my head, interesting little discoveries, and an unhealthy amount of nonsense.

The digital edition arrives on Sunday at 00:00 AM. Some editions are traditional, complete with stamps, paper, and the possibility of me becoming unnecessarily hateful of my handwriting. They have a specific time and ritual for delivery that you will know in later months of your life.

There is absolutely no subscription fee, no minimum reading requirement, and certainly no obligation to reply.

Think of it as a little postcard from my corner of the world. Something to open when you feel like it, smile at if it makes you smile, and put away when life gets busy.

The editorial department hopes you enjoy the occasional delivery.

Until next weekend,

Your friendly neighbourhood Nihal
Editor-in-Chief, The Nihal Weekly

P.S. Next week’s edition will be better. The management has made ambitious promises and is now legally obligated to produce something interesting.
